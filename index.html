Who we are

Ground truth, since before the grid.

NLC India Limited is a Navratna Government of India Enterprise headquartered in Neyveli, Tamil Nadu — a mining and power generation company that has spent nearly seven decades pulling lignite from the earth and turning it into the current that runs a nation. Today that same discipline is being pointed at the sun and the wind, with a firm roadmap toward gigawatt-scale green energy.

01

Mining

Open-cast lignite operations across multiple mines in Neyveli and Barsingsar, engineered for efficient, responsible extraction at national scale.

Power Generation

02

A fleet of thermal power stations converting mined lignite into dependable grid-scale electricity for southern and northern India.

03

Renewable Expansion

Gigawatt-scale solar capacity and a growing wind portfolio across Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Odisha and Assam — built for the decades after coal.

Seven decades, laid bare

The strata of our journey.

1956

Foundation

NLC is established to mine lignite in Neyveli, Tamil Nadu.

1960s–80s

Expansion of Mining Operations

Multiple open-cast mines commissioned to scale lignite output.

1980s–2000s

Thermal Power Growth

A growing fleet of thermal power stations built to convert lignite into grid electricity.

2010s

Renewable Energy Milestones

First solar and wind assets commissioned, marking a strategic diversification.

2020s

Solar Capacity Achievement

Gigawatt-scale solar capacity reached across multiple sites; 70 years of the enterprise celebrated.

Ahead

Future Green Energy Vision

Battery storage, green hydrogen, and continued renewable scale-up toward 20,130 MW by 2030.
